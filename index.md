I am **Yingtao Shen (沈颖涛)**, a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Science and Technology at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** (2022.09 – 2027.06 expected), affiliated with the **Efficient Computing Hardware and System Lab (ECHSL)**, advised by **Assoc. Prof. An Zou**. My research focuses on **efficient on-device AI**, including:

- **Dynamic inference & early exiting**: proactive exit prediction; hardware-software co-design for joint energy-latency optimization
- **Combinational model compression**: RL-based automatic search over quantization / pruning / distillation / early-exit pipelines
- **LLM inference acceleration**: training-free token-level seamless early exit with KV-cache sharing
- **Algorithm-hardware co-design**: inference system optimization for embedded GPUs and processors

My work has been published at **ACL, AAAI, DAC, DATE, and IEEE TCAD**, including four CCF-A papers as first / co-first author, with multiple **Oral** presentations and a **DAC Young Fellow Award**.

## Highlights

- **River-LLM** (ACL 2026, first author): training-free token-level seamless early exiting for decoder LLMs via KV-sharing exit branches, achieving **1.71×–2.16×** wall-clock speedup on math reasoning and code generation
- **Compression Space Search** (DATE 2026 **Oral**, first author): RL-searched combinational compression; **2.0–5.2×** inference speedup over TensorRT-optimized models on Jetson AGX Orin
- **LEAP** (IEEE TCAD 2025, first author): proactive early-exit prediction; **83%** energy reduction and **71%** latency reduction with < 1% accuracy loss
- **EENet** (DAC 2023 **Oral**, co-first author): co-design of dynamic early exit and DVFS; **64%** energy saving on ResNet
