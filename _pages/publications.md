## First / Co-first Author

**[ACL 2026] River-LLM: Large Language Model Seamless Exit Based on KV Share** (CCF-A, first author)

**Yingtao Shen**, An Zou.

A training-free, token-level seamless early-exit framework for decoder-only LLMs. KV-sharing exit branches eliminate expensive cache-restoration overhead, achieving **1.71×–2.16×** wall-clock speedup for Ministral3, Phi4, and Llama3 on math reasoning and code generation.

**[DATE 2026] Compression Space Search: RL-based Combinational Compression for Neural Networks** (CCF-B, **Oral**, first author)

**Yingtao Shen**, Yinchen Ni, Jiace Zhu, Jie Zhao, An Zou.

Models the selection, ordering, and degree of quantization, pruning, distillation, and early exiting as a Markov decision process, and searches optimal combinational compression policies with reinforcement learning — breaking the ceiling of any single method. Up to **100.8×** theoretical speedup on CNNs and ViTs; on Jetson AGX Orin, **2.0–5.2×** inference speedup and **3.3–5.4×** memory saving over TensorRT-optimized baselines.

**[IEEE TCAD 2025] LEAP: Light Weighting Neural Network Inference through Proactive Early Exiting Prediction** (CCF-A journal, first author)

**Yingtao Shen**, Xiangjie Li, Yehan Ma, Jie Zhao, An Zou.

A proactive early-exit prediction mechanism whose low-cost prediction engine determines the optimal computation depth early in inference. For CNNs and ViTs on Jetson AGX Orin and i7-10700: **83%** energy reduction, **71%** latency reduction, accuracy loss within 1%.

**[DAC 2023] EENet: Energy Efficient Neural Networks with Run-time Power Management** (CCF-A, **Oral**, co-first author)

Xiangjie Li\*, **Yingtao Shen\***, An Zou, Yehan Ma.

Co-design of dynamic early exiting and processor run-time power management (DVFS) for joint energy-performance optimization of NN inference. **64%** energy reduction on ResNet while beating SOTA on real-time performance.

**[CSTIC 2023] Learning-Based Performance and Power Model for Processor Microsecond DVFS** (**Oral**, first author)

**Yingtao Shen**, An Zou.
